# Systems

## System

* [LMS](/_systems/lms.md) - The learning management system where courses and course materials live.
* [NetSuite](/_systems/netsuite.md) - The ERP for the general ledger, receivables, payables, and payroll journals.
* [Salesforce](/_systems/salesforce.md) - The CRM for applicants and students, including enrollment records.
* [SIS](/_systems/sis.md) - The student information system: the record of courses, grades, and student accounts.
